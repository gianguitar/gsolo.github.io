About Gian

Born in the Philippines and raised in Los Angeles, Gian Solo is a singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist creating music rooted in rock, folk, and alternative sounds.

After years of performing, recording, and playing in bands, Gian continues to build his own sound as an independent artist. He writes, sings, and performs the guitars and bass on his music, recording, producing, mixing, and mastering his projects himself.

His latest release, Over & Over, is a five-song EP featuring songs about love, connection, and the ups and downs of being human. The release reflects his hands-on approach to making music: personal songwriting, guitar-driven arrangements, and a sound shaped entirely around his own creative vision.

Through his music, Gian hopes to connect with people, bring a little comfort and energy into their lives, and create songs that stay with them long after the music stops.
