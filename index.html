About Gian

Born in the Philippines and raised in Los Angeles, Gian Solo creates music that gives life, comfort, and energy back to the listener. With a background spanning years of performing, recording, and touring with multiple bands, Gian now writes, sings, and plays all instruments—guitars, bass, and vocals—on his self-produced tracks. Gian’s 2026 self-produced album, set to release this year, dives deep into the themes of love, joy, breakups, life purpose, existential questions, and the human experience. He also edited and mastered the entire album himself, showcasing his dedication to fully realizing his musical vision. Known for his authentic songwriting, commanding performances, and lush acoustic-rock arrangements, Gian blends the best parts of folk, rock, and alternative music to tell stories that connect and inspire. His previous singles, including “Sunsets,” “Stay” and “Come Back to Me,” highlight his honest, emotional lyricism and nuanced, heartfelt instrumentation. Through his music, Gian aims to inspire, empower, and connect people, while fostering positivity, inclusivity, and human connection, giving back in every note he plays.
